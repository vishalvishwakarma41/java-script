Rules & Regulation

  • Ensure You Have A Stable Internet Connection Before Starting The Exam.
  • Use A Desktop Or Laptop- Mobile Phones And Tablets Are Not Allowed.
  • Open The Exam Only In Google Chrome Or Mozilla Firefox (Latest Version).
  • Keep Your Webcam And Microphone ON If Monitoring Is Required.
  • Do Not Refresh Or Close The Exam Window Once It Has Started.
  • The Exam Is Time-Bound, So Complete All Sections Within The Given Duration.
  • Do Not Switch Tabs Or Open New Windows During The Exam.
  • Copying, Screen Sharing, Or Usin Al/Code Generation Tools Is Strictly Prohibited.
  • Do Not Discuss Or Share Answers With Others During The Exam.
  • Avoid Multiple Logins Or Using More Than One Device At A Time.
  • Review Your Answers Carefully Before Final Submission- You Can't Edit Afterward.
  • Wait For The Submission Confirmation Message Before Closing The Window.
  • Exam Results Will Be Shared Later Via The Official Portal Or Registered Email.
  • Maintain Honesty And Discipline - Violations May Result In Disqualification.
