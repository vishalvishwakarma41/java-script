Rules & Regulation

  1. Ensure You Have A Stable Internet Connection Before Starting The Exam.
  2. Use A Desktop Or Laptop- Mobile Phones And Tablets Are Not Allowed.
  3. Open The Exam Only In Google Chrome Or Mozilla Firefox (Latest Version).
  4. Keep Your Webcam And Microphone ON If Monitoring Is Required.
  5. Do Not Refresh Or Close The Exam Window Once It Has Started.
  6. The Exam Is Time-Bound, So Complete All Sections Within The Given Duration.
  7. Do Not Switch Tabs Or Open New Windows During The Exam.
  8. 8. Copying, Screen Sharing, Or Using Al/Code Generation Tools Is Strictly Prohibited.
  9. Do Not Discuss Or Share Answers With Others During The Exam.
  10. Avoid Multiple Logins Or Using More Than One Device At A Time.
  11. Review Your Answers Carefully Before Final Submission- You Can't Edit Afterward.
  12. Wait For The Submission Confirmation Message Before Closing The Window.
  13. Exam Results Will Be Shared Later Via The Official Portal Or Registered Email.
  14. Maintain Honesty And Discipline - Violations May Result In Disqualification.
